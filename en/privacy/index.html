Privacy policy

This policy explains what information FORGE collects through kurant.ca, why, and how to exercise your rights.

Who we are

Kurant is a product of FORGE, a business based in Montreal, Quebec. The person in charge of protecting personal information is the founder of FORGE. For any question or request: bonjour@kurant.ca.

What we collect

Only what you enter in the waitlist form:

  • your email address;
  • your bike's brand and model, the system and voltage chosen in the compatibility tool, and the controller label text if you provide it;
  • your phone type (Android or iPhone) and your province;
  • the date of your consent and the version of the text you accepted.

If you confirm your address, we also keep:

  • the confirmation date, your founder number and your referral code;
  • the code of the person who invited you, if any;
  • your answer to "will you back it on day one?", if you answer it.

To stop abuse of the form, we also keep for 24 hours a one-way fingerprint (a hash) of your IP address, never the address itself.

The site uses no advertising cookies and no tracking tools.

Why

  • To tell you when Kurant is available for your bike, and about the launch and the Kickstarter campaign.
  • To decide which bikes and phones to support first.
  • To confirm the address is yours (double opt-in), and to count the friends who signed up through your link.
  • To publish anonymous totals on the site: the number of confirmed sign-ups and how they split by bike system ("the brand race"). No name or address is ever published.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for other purposes without your consent. If you back the campaign, Kickstarter handles your pledge under its own privacy policy.

Where your information is kept

The site and its database are hosted by Cloudflare, whose servers may be located outside Quebec. Emails are sent by Resend, a provider based in the United States, which receives your email address and language to send our messages and handle unsubscribes. Only the designated person in charge has access to this information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information until you unsubscribe, or at most 24 months after Kurant launches. It is then deleted. A sign-up that is never confirmed is deleted 30 days after the last confirmation email.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct or delete your information, and withdraw your consent. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. Write to bonjour@kurant.ca; we reply within 30 days. You can also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.
