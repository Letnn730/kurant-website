The steps

How a brand becomes compatible

Every brand goes through the same four steps. In the app, a setting only opens once its encoding has been measured on a real controller, at step 3.

  1. Researched

    The system is studied from its documentation and owners' reports. Nothing is measured yet.

  2. On the bench

    A real controller is connected to the test bench. What it exchanges with the display is recorded with a logic analyzer.

  3. Bench-verified

    Every reading and every setting is checked on the controller. The brand then becomes available, with its cable.

  4. Road-confirmed

    The first riders confirm it works on their own bikes.

Before you choose

Find your system

The bike's brand isn't always enough: two models from the same brand can use different systems. These clues help you find yours.

  • Display

    The display model

    It's often printed on the display or on its back: KT-LCD3, LCD5, LCD8, VM-T8, S866…

  • Connector

    The connector's shape

    On Bafang, the display cable's connector tells you the system: round with 5 pins, or triangular.

  • Label

    The controller's label

    It shows the controller's brand and model, for example KT36/48SVPRL. If in doubt, copy it into the sign-up form.

  • Battery

    The battery voltage

    It's printed on the battery or the charger. This version of the module is designed for 48 and 52 V batteries.
